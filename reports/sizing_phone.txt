✅ Inverse volatility wins (strategies that swing more get fewer lots)
At 2 lots average:
• Equal 2 lots each: +₹13.12L · worst drop −₹1.68L
• Inverse volatility: +₹13.13L · worst drop −₹1.38L
Same profit, 18% smaller worst drop (return/drop 9.5 vs 7.8)

Suggested lots at a 2-lot level (updated monthly):
EMA SENSEX 1 · PSAR SENSEX 1 · Judas 3 · ORB 2 · Pivot NIFTY 2 · Pivot SENSEX 2 · Prev-zone 2 · Failbreak 2

Other methods:
• Risk parity: no better than equal
• Drawdown brake (halve after a big fall): smaller drops but less profit; pausing at 1 lot cost more than half the profit
• Volatility targeting (cut size in jumpy periods): worse (return/drop 5.4)

At today's 1 lot each, nothing to change: lots can't be split. Use it when you scale up to 2+ lots.
