❌ Selling iron flies in "quiet" windows: loses
NIFTY 2026: 323 trades, −₹67.8k · SENSEX: 402 trades, −₹94.1k
The quiet signal DID help a little: avg −₹210 vs −₹230 per trade when placed at fixed times, win 22% vs 17%.

Why it loses: costs. An iron fly is 4 options = 8 orders per trade.
Brokerage ₹160 + taxes + slippage ≈ ₹230 per trade, but an hour of time decay earns only about that much. Intraday, hour-long selling can't beat the costs at 1 lot.

❌ Filter for our 7 buying strategies: makes it worse
Keeping only high big-move trades would have cut 2025 profit by ₹84k–1.2L, so it was not used.

Bottom line on dhan_prob so far:
The model really knows when markets will be busy or quiet (AUC 0.69), but every way of trading it intraday loses to option pricing (buying) or costs (selling).

One fair last test: a "quiet DAY" version – predict at 09:30 whether the whole day will be calm, sell one iron fly and hold to 15:15. One trade a day = costs paid once, a full day of time decay.
